Privacy Policy — Keskikout (subscription tracker)

Last updated: August 26, 2026

In short: Keskikout collects no personal data. The app runs entirely on your device, with no user account and no server.

1. Data stored by the app

The information you enter (subscriptions, amounts, billing cycles, renewal and cancellation dates, notes) is stored only on your device, in a local database (SQLite). It is neither collected nor transmitted to the developer or to third parties. There is no user account and no server. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

2. Notifications

Reminders ahead of your cancellation deadlines are local notifications, scheduled and triggered on your device from the dates you entered. No notification is sent from a server.

3. In-app purchase

The only processing carried out by third parties concerns the in-app purchase, handled through Google Play and our purchase processor RevenueCat. In that context, an anonymous technical identifier and transaction details are processed by these services under their own policies. Keskikout never has access to your payment information.

4. Cancellation guides

The catalogue of cancellation guides ships inside the app: reading it requires no connection and sends no request. If you open the website of a service from a guide, the page opens in the browser of your device and is then covered by the privacy policy of that service.

5. Advertising and tracking

Keskikout contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

6. Children

Keskikout collects no data from anyone, including minors.

7. Changes

Any update to this policy will be published on this page.
