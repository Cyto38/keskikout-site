Frequently asked questions

What is Keskikout for?

Keskikout brings your subscriptions together in one place and shows what they add up to per month and per year, based on the amounts and billing cycles you enter.

How do I stop missing a cancellation deadline?

For each subscription, you enter the renewal date and the notice period. Keskikout schedules an alert on your phone ahead of every deadline.

Does Keskikout explain how to cancel?

Yes. The app ships with verified cancellation guides for French services: where to go, what to prepare, which steps to follow.

Is my data private?

Yes. Keskikout runs entirely on your phone, with no account and no server. Your subscriptions, amounts and dates never leave your device.
